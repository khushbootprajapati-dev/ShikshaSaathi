      STAY SAFE ONLINE

      Cyber Safety

      Follow these simple tips to stay safe on the internet.

      🔐 Never share your OTP or password.

      Example: A caller says your bank account will be blocked unless you tell them an OTP. Stop and call your bank using its official number.

      🔗 Avoid clicking suspicious links.

      Example: A message promises a free phone or prize and asks you to click a strange link. Do not open it or share details.

      🔑 Use strong and unique passwords.

      Use a long passphrase with words, numbers and symbols. Do not reuse your banking password on other sites.

      🛡️ Keep your personal information private.

      Do not post your address, school ID, bank details or live location publicly.

      🙋 Report online problems to a trusted adult.

      If someone pressures or threatens you online, save the message and ask a trusted adult for help.
